Frequently asked questions

Answers about Aza
Akaunting

  • What is Aza Akaunting?

    Aza Akaunting is a business management system that brings point of sale, inventory, purchasing, customer records, staff tools, and reporting together.

  • How much does Aza Akaunting cost?

    The 4 User Plan is TZS 25,000 per month. The 6 User Plan is TZS 40,000 per month.

  • What is a user in a plan?

    A user is a person with an account to access Aza Akaunting. Choose a plan whose user limit can accommodate the people on your team.

  • Can I manage inventory and purchasing?

    Yes. Aza includes product catalogues, item kits, barcode tools, stock counts, locations, transfers, supplier records, purchase orders, and receiving workflows.

  • How do I open the app?

    Select Open App or Get Started to go to the Aza Akaunting application.

  • What features are included?

    Both plans include all Aza Akaunting features, including sales and POS, items and inventory, purchasing, customers, payments, expenses, projects, employees, payroll, reports, and store operations.

  • What is the difference between the two plans?

    The plans include the same features. The TZS 25,000 plan supports up to 4 users and 2 stores. The TZS 40,000 plan supports up to 6 users and 4 stores.

  • What is a store in a plan?

    A store is a business location managed in Aza Akaunting. The 4 User Plan includes up to 2 stores, and the 6 User Plan includes up to 4 stores.

  • Does Aza include payroll and projects?

    Yes. Both plans include employee records, salary structures, payroll, payslips, time clock attendance, and project tracking.
