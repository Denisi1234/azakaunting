Explore every feature

Aza brings point of sale, inventory, purchasing, customer management, staff tools, and financial reporting together. Explore each area below to see how the tools support everyday work across your stores.

Sales & Point of Sale

Serve customers and record every transaction from a single sales workflow. Aza supports counter sales as well as the documents and follow-up processes around them.

  • Point of sale checkout and sales entry
  • Invoices, quotes, and receipts
  • Returns and suspended sales
  • Work orders and delivery notes
  • Sales price approval workflows

Sales are connected to customer records, payments, and inventory so teams can keep a consistent view of each transaction.

Inventory & Purchasing

Keep the product catalogue and stock movement organized across your business. Track what is available, where it is held, and what needs replenishing.

  • Item catalogue, item kits, and item attributes
  • Barcode label and barcode sheet tools
  • Stock counts, inventory adjustments, and stock transfer workflows
  • Multiple stock locations and stores
  • Supplier records, purchase orders, and purchase approvals
  • Receiving goods and purchase receiving records

Use purchasing and receiving workflows to record incoming stock and maintain clearer inventory records.

Customers & Payments

Keep customer information close to sales activity and give staff a clear view of account history and payment records.

  • Customer profiles, account balances, and credit limits
  • Discounts, loyalty rewards, and gift cards
  • Multiple payment types and payment records
  • Payment reconciliation tools
  • Customer messaging and communication records

Customer and payment tools help teams understand who they are serving and how transactions are being settled.

Operations & Finance

Coordinate the people and processes behind your stores, then review activity through operational and financial reports.

  • Expenses and expense categories
  • Projects with linked sales and costs
  • Employee records, salary structures, payroll, and payslips
  • Time clock attendance and employee reports
  • Sales, receiving, inventory, expense, tax, and graphical reports
  • Accounting tools, multi-currency support, and tax setup
  • Register shifts, cashups, notifications, bookmarks, and audit history

These tools help owners and managers monitor activity and keep routine administration connected to daily operations.

Every feature is included in both Aza plans. Choose the plan based on the number of users and stores your team needs.
