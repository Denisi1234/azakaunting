About Aza

One connected business system

Run your daily work
with Aza Akaunting.

Sales, stock, people, and reporting in one place.

Aza Akaunting is a point of sale and business management platform for teams that need to coordinate everyday business activity. It brings checkout, inventory, purchasing, customer accounts, expenses, projects, staff tools, and reports into one system.

Instead of handling each task in isolation, teams can connect sales to stock, purchasing to receiving, and business activity to useful reports. The result is a clearer view of operations across the stores you manage.
